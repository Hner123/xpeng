# X9 showcase images

Reference: https://www.xpeng.com/ph/model/X9 (accessed September 5, 2026).

## Interactive rotation

Official X9 sprite, referenced by the page's `schemaRotateCar` data:
https://s-cdn.xpeng.com/commoncms/prod/2026-05-11/5d1550779dfa4a669b37f202bde06e42.png?name=X9-360-mob

The 27000 × 350 transparent strip contains 36 views at 750 × 350. `x9-360.webp` repacks these into a 6 × 6 grid (4500 × 2100), loaded automatically as the showcase approaches the viewport. `x9-360-poster.webp` is frame 29, displayed while loading and if JavaScript or the rotation asset is unavailable. These official studio renders depict the vehicle supplied in that asset; they do not promise a specific local trim.

- Exterior: https://s-cdn.xpeng.com/commoncms/prod/2026-03-19/b30d6c297774435298a77bd77dba51f3.jpg
- Meteorite Black cabin: https://s-cdn.xpeng.com/commoncms/prod/2026-05-08/8eef94e992ed43d6bf484ba3869c5297.jpg

The `exterior-*` and `cabin-*` WebP files are resized crops of these official XPENG images, supplied locally at 480 and 800 pixels wide. The exterior crop removes the source image's white lower fade. The existing `x9-*` sunset images are campaign assets.

Feature descriptions follow the official Philippines model page. Existing reservation pricing, promotion dates, and charging figures in the reservation panel were retained from the campaign content; this edit does not independently verify those offers.
