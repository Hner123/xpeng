# L03 showcase

Source page: https://www.xpeng.com/ph/model/L03 (accessed September 10, 2026).

Official rotation asset referenced by the page's `schemaRotateCar` data:
https://s-cdn.xpeng.com/commoncms/prod/2026-07-07/9a2201f925b54a98ae90449b616e8318.png?name=L03_360_PC

The 43200 x 560 strip contains 36 angles, each 1200 x 560 pixels.
`l03-360.webp` repacks the angles into a 6 x 6 grid (7200 x 3360).
`l03-360-poster.webp` contains angle 29 as the loading/no-JavaScript fallback.
The sprite loads when the L03 panel becomes visible near the viewport.

Copy summarizes the official model page's coupe design, storage, and interior.
No L03 price or reservation promotion is asserted. The CTA links to XPENG's
official L03 page for current specifications and availability.
