FAQ

Before you ask.

Is the launch event really free?
Yes, XPENG's Driving Into A New Day Physical AI Open House is completely free to attend, by invitation only. You'll never be asked for payment to register, claim your tickets, or enter the event.
How do I get an invitation?
Join the waitlist above. Invitations are limited, with guests selected from the waitlist. You'll receive a personal claim code by email and SMS to claim your free ticket through SM Tickets. Your SM Ticket is your entry pass for the show at MOA Arena.
Who gets selected, and how does the waitlist work?
Guests are selected from the waitlist by our team, based on factors such as city of residence and the profile information you share in Step 2. Register early and complete both steps for the best chance to get an invite.
When will I know if I'm invited?
Invitations go out about a week before the event. If selected, you'll receive your invitation and claim code via email and SMS, so make sure your contact details upon registration are accurate.
How do I claim my ticket via SM Tickets? (72-Hour Rule)
Your invitation will include a link to XPENG's Driving Into A New Day page on SM Tickets. Simply enter your claim code to secure your free ticket, no payment required. You'll have 72 hours to claim your tickets before the code expires and your seat is offered to the next guest on the waitlist.
Can I bring a companion or plus one?
Each invitation and SM Ticket is strictly valid for one (1) person only. If family members or friends wish to join you, we encourage them to sign up for the waitlist individually using their personal details.
Can I transfer my invitation or ticket to someone else?
No. Invitations and tickets are personal and non-transferable, matched with your registered name and phone number upon registration. Please bring a valid government-issued ID with your SM Tickets pass.
What happens if I don't get selected for an invitation?
All registered guests on the waitlist remain in our priority circle. Even if you aren't selected for the September 25 launch event, your registration grants you priority access to upcoming XPENG experiences and test drives.
What is the event schedule for the day?
We will air the event live on XPENG Philippines' social media channels at 3:30 PM, while the event officially opens at 6:00 PM for interactive experiences, the IRON humanoid showcase, and play zone games, leading into the main arena show and headline concert.
What do I need to present upon event venue entry?
Please present your official claimed SM Ticket along with one (1) valid government-issued ID matching the registered name on your invitation. The original registration QR on your phone should be kept as it serves as your digital games pass in the play zones.
Is there an age policy?
Guests 18 and above may register and attend on their own. Minors are welcome only when accompanied by a registered adult guest, and each attendee still needs their own claimed ticket.
Parking, transit, and venue drop-off
MOA Arena is located within the SM Mall of Asia complex in Pasay City, with nearby parking and access via ride-hailing and bus drop-off points. The Foyer opens at 6:00 PM, and we recommend arriving early to allow for traffic concerns and entry procedures. Detailed arrival and parking information will be sent to confirmed guests the day before the event.
What can I experience and win in the foyer?
The Foyer Play Zone opens at 6:00 PM, with instant prizes including exclusive XPENG tote bags and keychains, plus chances to win special merchandise. The original registration QR on your phone should be kept as it serves as your digital games pass in the play zones.
How is my personal data handled?
Your data is collected to manage your Driving Into A New Day registration, and with your consent, to connect you with an authorized XPENG dealer near you. You can withdraw consent or request your information to be deleted at any time. Please see full details and contacts via the Data Privacy Notice.
