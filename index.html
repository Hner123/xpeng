FAQ

Before you ask.

Is it really free?
Yes — completely free, by invitation. There is nothing to buy anywhere on this page or at the door. Anyone asking you to pay for entry is not us.
How do I get in?
Join the waitlist above. Invitations are limited and curated from the waitlist. Selected registrants receive a personal claim code by email and SMS, with a link to claim their free ticket on SM Tickets — that SM ticket is your entry pass to MOA Arena.
When will I know if I'm invited?
Invitations are released about one week before the event. Watch your inbox and your SMS — the code is sent to the email and mobile number you register with, so please keep them accurate.
What is the SM Tickets step?
Your invitation links to the SM Tickets page for Future Night. You enter your single-use code there and claim a free ticket — no payment, no fees. You have 72 hours from the invitation; after that the code expires and the seat is released to the next names on the waitlist.
Who gets picked, and how?
Selection is made from the waitlist by our team, guided by city or residence and the profile answers in Step 2 of the form. Registering early and completing both steps gives you the strongest chance.
Can I transfer my ticket?
No — invitations are personal and matched to your registered name and mobile number. Bring a valid ID together with your SM ticket.
Is there an age policy?
Guests 18 and above may register and attend on their own. Minors are welcome only when accompanied by a registered adult guest, and each attendee still needs their own claimed ticket.
Parking and how to get there
MOA Arena sits inside the SM Mall of Asia complex in Pasay, served by mall parking and the usual ride-hailing and bus drop-off points. The foyer opens at 6:00 PM — arrive early, gates and traffic around the complex get heavy before a full-house show. Detailed arrival instructions go out to claimed guests the day before.
What can I win?
The foyer play zone runs from 6:00 PM with instant prizes — exclusive XPENG tote bags, keychains and a special-merch draw. The personal code in your invitation is your game pass, so keep that email or SMS handy on the night.
What happens to my information?
Your data is used to manage this event and, only if you tick that consent, to connect you with an authorized XPENG dealer near you. You can withdraw consent or request deletion any time — full details and contacts are in the Data Privacy notice.

Registration takes under a minute and closes when the Arena is full.
